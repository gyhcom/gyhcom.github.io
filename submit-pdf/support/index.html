FAQ

How do I create a PDF?

Open Submit PDF, tap Scan Document or Import Photos, adjust the pages, then continue to export and share.

Why can I not export more PDFs?

Free use includes a limited number of exports. Submit PDF Pro unlocks unlimited submission-ready PDF exports.

How do I restore a previous purchase?

Open the Pro screen from the Home screen and tap Restore Purchases.

Where are my scanned documents stored?

Submit PDF stores exported documents locally on your device until you remove them from the app or from your device storage.
