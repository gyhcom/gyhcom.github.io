Submit PDF Privacy Policy

Submit PDF is designed as an offline-first document utility. The app helps you scan or import document photos, improve readability, create PDFs, and share them from your device.

Last updated: April 23, 2026

Documents and Photos

Your scanned documents, imported photos, generated PDFs, and recent document history are processed and stored locally on your device. Submit PDF does not upload your documents or photos to a GyhLabs server.

Purchases

Submit PDF Pro purchases are processed through Apple in-app purchase. The app uses RevenueCat to manage purchase status and entitlement restoration. RevenueCat may process purchase-related identifiers and receipt information needed to provide the Pro unlock.

Support Email

If you contact support by email, we receive the email address and message content you choose to send. We use that information only to respond to your request.

Analytics and Accounts

  • No account or sign-in is required to use Submit PDF.
  • The app does not include cloud sync.
  • The app does not use a GyhLabs backend to store your documents.
  • Basic in-app diagnostics may be logged locally by the operating system.

Contact
