Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 13, 2026

Submit PDF is developed by GyhLabs. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app uses and how that information is handled.

1. Information the app uses

  • Camera access when you choose to scan a document.
  • Photo library access when you choose to import photos.
  • Files you create in the app, such as exported PDFs and recent document records.
  • Purchase-related data required to process and restore in-app purchases.

2. How document data is handled

Submit PDF is designed to work primarily on-device. Scanned pages, imported images, generated PDFs, and recent document records are stored locally on your device unless you choose to share them through Apple's system share sheet.

Submit PDF does not require an account and does not provide cloud sync in the current version of the app.

3. In-app purchases

Submit PDF uses Apple's in-app purchase infrastructure and RevenueCat to manage purchase state, entitlement status, and restoration of purchases. Purchase-related information may be processed by Apple and RevenueCat for these purposes.

4. Sharing

When you use the share feature, the selected PDF is handed off through Apple's share sheet to the destination you choose. Handling of shared files by third-party apps or services is governed by those services' policies.

5. Data retention

Documents and recent history remain on your device until you delete them from the app or remove the app and its data from your device.

6. Your choices

  • You can deny camera or photo access in iOS Settings.
  • You can delete exported files and recent documents from your device.
  • You can contact support with privacy-related questions.
