Today's Room Privacy Policy

Today's Room is designed as a local-first iOS focus timer. Your focus items and settings stay on your device.

Last updated: July 8, 2026

Focus Items and Settings

Your focus items, focus duration settings, selected room theme, and local completion state are stored locally on your device. If you choose a Personal Room photo, that photo and its local framing data are also stored on your device. Today's Room does not upload your focus items, settings, or selected photo to a GyhLabs server.

Personal Room Photos

Today's Room may ask for photo library access when you choose a Personal Room photo. The selected photo is copied into the app's local storage and used only as a focus background.

The app may analyze the selected image on your device to choose a calmer crop position. This analysis is local-only. The photo and framing result are not uploaded to Today's Room, GyhLabs, or any external server.

Purchases

Today's Room is sold as a paid app through the App Store. Payment processing is handled by Apple. Today's Room does not receive or store your payment card information.

Analytics and Accounts

  • No account or sign-in is required to use Today's Room.
  • The app does not include cloud sync.
  • The app does not use a GyhLabs backend to store your focus data.
  • The app does not include third-party analytics or advertising tracking in the first release.
  • Basic app diagnostics may be logged locally by the operating system.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not require an account to use the app.
  • We do not upload your focus items or timer history to a GyhLabs server.
  • We do not upload your selected Personal Room photo to a GyhLabs server.
  • We do not run cloud sync for your focus data.
  • We do not sell personal data.

Support Email

If you contact support by email, we receive the email address and message content you choose to send. We use that information only to respond to your request.
