Today's Room Support

Today's Room is a calm iOS focus timer designed to stay open on your desk while you work.

If you need help with focus sessions, task editing, room themes, screen awake behavior, or App Store purchase issues, contact us by email.

Email: gyeonho@gmail.com

Before You Contact Support

  • Make sure your device is updated to a recent iOS version.
  • Restart the app and try the same focus session again.
  • If the screen does not stay awake, check Low Power Mode and device settings.

  • Focus timer pause, resume, complete, and stop behavior
