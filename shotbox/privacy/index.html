ShotBox Privacy Policy

ShotBox is designed as a local macOS utility. The app watches folders you select, helps rename screenshots, and moves files into destination folders you choose.

Last updated: April 23, 2026

Screenshots and Files

Screenshots and file paths are processed locally on your Mac. ShotBox does not upload your screenshots or files to a GyhLabs server.

Folder Access

ShotBox stores local preferences needed to remember your selected watch folder and destination folders. macOS may use security-scoped bookmarks so the app can access folders you selected after relaunch.

Data Collection

  • No account or sign-in is required to use ShotBox.
  • ShotBox does not include cloud sync.
  • ShotBox does not use advertising tracking.
  • ShotBox does not sell personal data.

Support Email

If you contact support by email, we receive the email address and message content you choose to send. We use that information only to respond to your request.

Contact

If you have privacy questions, contact gyeonho@gmail.com.
